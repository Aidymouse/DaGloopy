
# I can't escape boss fights

B- b- b- boss battle!

A brain worm lodged firmly in place by the 5th edition tradition is the idea of a culmination to the action. When I'm preparing a dungeon, a scenario, and adventure, I feel this pang of wrongness if there isn't rising action to some kind of climax. Given that I play D&D type games, the climax of teh adventure is usually a boss fight of some kind. This particularly pains me while thinking about how to run a sandbox, particularly a city sandbox.

This feeling that the whole thing is pointless and aimless keeps chewing away at me. Every time I try to prepare something I get hit with this pang of uselessness.
