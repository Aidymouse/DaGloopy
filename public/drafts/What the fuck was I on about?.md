# What the fuck was I on about? ( CHECK TODOS before publish )

Hello intelligent creatures,

I started this blog because I felt like good RPG guys had blogs. That's kind of the truth of it. This aesthetics driven approach to old school RPGing is, as it happens, not uncommon. I got swept right up in broad ideals that are starting to be looked at with a more critical eye ( TODO (combat as war?)[], etc)

I wrote a lot of posts, not off of any sort of argumentative substance, but pretty much as confirmations of what I already believed. But over time I've grown to look at things a bit more pragmatically. My dogma has turned down. And I thought it would be fun to go back and review those old articles of mine that I think are vapid in group pandering and see if the arguments hold water, or try to state more precisely my meaning in each post. Not just to refresh myself on what I used to believe, but to see if I can apply some more rigorous reasoning to my tastes.

## The Future

You know for a blog of like 15 posts there sure is a lot of meta commentary going on.

I kind of don't know what will happen to this blog. I keep the diaries updated mostly for me and my friends, and I think I'd like to post game material here. I don't think I'll post a lot of theory stuff because I don't think I have the sauce to contribute anything meaty to the conversation, and I lack the time to do my due diligence to make good posts.
