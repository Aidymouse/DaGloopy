# Occluse Playtest - Ajira II

## Session Report

At the mouth of the cave, **Zazu**, **Goda**, **Greek** and **Tanawe** hear slithering, like a grindsheet slab being dragged over stone. The sun has sunk below the horizon, but the bright stars and full moon illuminate the mountain pass with a faint silver light. Fearing the snakes approach, **Zazu** declares they should join the others in the cave. As they go, a monstrous shape darts across the path, constricting **Tanawe** and trying to drag him off the mountain.

**Zazu** acts quickly, summoning his magical strength and zapping the snake with a jolt of lightning! **Tanawe** is freed and the four flee down the passage. Before they do, **Zazu** conjures another spell and closes the tunnel with jagged spikes of rock. The snakes fervent attempts to smash through the stone echo around them as they run.

Circumstances are grim in the cavern. There is no way out, especially now that **Zazu** closed the entrance. **Ŧaki** realizes that the mossy ceiling must indicate that there is water somewhere above. **Zazu** casts a spell to draw some of the earth down, but he is inflicted with chaos, instead creating a rubbery sack that droops the ceiling downwards. This bulbous sack now drips a slow trickle of water into the bowl. **Ŧaki** exacerbates this into a small fall by prodding it with his stick.

With no other options, **Kadian Oth** takes guard position on the door and the group await the snakes entrance. When the banging stops, a strange quiet settles over the cavern.

The silence breaks as the snake lunges from the mouth of the cave. **Kadian** jumps over it, plunging down with is wakizashi, but it is deflected entirely by the snakes hard scales. The snake slithers into the bowl, it's long body whipping out to strike and constrict the fighters. The snake pours from the entrance without end, blocking the only way out.

The characters engage in a brutal fight. **Greek** is the first casualty, killed when the snake bites clean into her eye. It's venom seeps into her flesh and kills her. **Ryuku** spots a katana sticking out of one body segment of the snake, and rips it free, opening a wound that **Kadian** can use to land several blows. **Kadian** gets sucked into the churning mass of coils, but his armor keeps him alive.

**Zazu** is also killed, his leg destroyed by the crushing snakes coils. **Kenji** tries to stab the snakes eyes, but his sai is deflected by the snakes armored eyelids. By now the snake has fully emerged into the cavern, leaving the entrance unguarded. **Ŧaki**, **Keppi** and **Ryuku** flee the cave (not before throwing **Kadian** a stick), following shortly by **Tanawe** and **Kenji**, who makes a daring dash across the top of the snakes body to escape. **Kadian** manages to free himself. He stays momentarily to help **Goda** but is urged to flee. **Goda** stays behind and distracts the snake so his compatriots can escape. He manages to destroy one of the snakes eyes with his whip before having his spine broken by the snakes constricting coils.

At the mouth of the cave, the characters find that sharp rocks have coated the entrance of the cave - a byproduct of **Zazu**s earlier spell. Unfortunately, the party are in pitch black when this occurs. **Ŧaki** faceplants on the rocks, taking damage. The party do overcome the rocks and flee down the mountain pass. They pass the ritually slain camel, now just a pile of viscera, and continue down the pass the way they had not gone before. Overcome with exhaustion, the characters fall into an uneasy sleep.

**Ryuku** wakes first, hearing the distant sound of footsteps and conversation. He spies, then alerts the party to the presence of an old woman and a small boy making their way up the pass. These hungry poor folk wear nothing but grey rags. When they meet, the old women questions where the party came from. When she sees the katana **Ryuku** wrested from the giant snake , she wails, shouting "You have stolen from **Tanagiba**, protector of the mountain! You are accursed!"

The party quickly learn that these people are from a tribe called the mountain men. Further questioning reveals that the men are impartial towards the Shadow-men, and even sell them escaped victims. Because of this, the party detain the boy and grandmother, so the mountain men might not know their presence. Following the urgent warnings from **Gita** the elder, the party venture back up the pass to return the sword to **Tanagiba**.

At the mouth of the cave, **Ryuku** offers the sword and **Gita** calls the snake. It emerges from its cave, one eye destroyed and the other scarred. **Gita** weeps at her damaged deity, which promptly opens its mouth and devours her. **Ryuku** dodges a bite and flees with the rest of his party down the pass. The boy, frozen with fear, is next in line to be eaten, but he is saved by **Kadian**, who picks up the boy and flees. He realises that the snake has a distaste for sunlight and stops his flight at a turn in the mountain pass where the morning sun shines bright. **Tanagiba** does not pursue them past the sun line. It is finally driven away by **Ŧaki**s mirror shining light into it's eyes.

The characters continue down the pass and make their way into a valley full of wildflowers, home of the mountain men tribe. The tribe spots them and a detachment of 6 led by **Gozu**, brother to the boy **Hitei** venture over to negotiate. The conversation is tense. **Gozu** believes the party to be fleeing prisoners of the shadow men, and murderers of **Gita**. As the tension rises, **Tanawe**, a former slave, chooses to fight rather than be taken prisoner again. The party quickly dispatch **Gozu** and parade his head up high on **Ryuku**s katana. This pacifies the mountain men, who allow the party to proceed through the valley so long as they do no more killing. The party take this chance and continue their journey through the mountains, leaving enemies behind them.

 - One player praised the unconstrained feeling the combat system had, which made me glad. There was a little bit of weirdness in players working together, but I've written a little bit of guidance on joint actions to handle this idea.
    - One thing of note was a player wanted to enchant a characters sword with lightning, but chose not to because his failed roll could have given the snake a chance to move. I don't _really_ mind because it was a tactical decision, but part of me thought it was maybe a misstep that a player had a cool idea and the rules incentivised them to not do it. If I were to handle this situation again I'd rely on the joint action rules to ensure him that his cool combo action would happen before an enemy reaction could disrupt it.

 - We had a magic user using the new magic rules I cooked up. I think these are finally just right. Parsing chaos is a little more tricky this go around but I like how it warps the effect of the spell rather than just giving the caster an unrelated mutation. Minor chaos was also pretty interesting. The 'basic spell' idea was a little hard to grasp at first but I think it's simple enough once the players become more familiar with the spell effect list.
