# Occluse Playtest - Ajira I

The previous playtest determined a lot about the direction I want to take Occluse, so I spent a couple weeks writing up a reformed system, uncreatively titled Occluse Again Again. This session used the [October 4 Playtest Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1IvuMgMTtVYImK4_1JUr_GbGfVGdbjd3vJ570dk_pJl8/edit?tab=t.0)

## Session Report

Under the sweltering desert sun, a procession of 3 caravans move like ants over the sand. A group of 30 shadow men, skin as cold as stone, some mounted atop rams, guard the caravans on their journey to The Black Pyramid, which looms on the horizon. The prisoners inside were kidnapped from their homes. Some have been travelling for months. Their destination is finally on the horizon... but for what purpose?

The prisoners are:

 - **Keppi**, a wanderer
 - **Ŧaki**, a hermit who lived with the ant men of the desert
 - **Kenji**, a Telexu Face-dancer
 - **Kadian Oth**, a trained Ibis Warrior
 - **Ryuku**, a Ronin

 Plus **Zazu**, **Godo**, **Tanawe**, and **Greek**.

There is a 10th prisoner in the cart, one who has been muttering quiet prayers since the day he was put in. A shadow-man begins picking a fight with **Keppi**, but a spear thrust is deflected just in time by **Kadian**'s chained wrists. Before the conflict can escalate, the praying prisoner yells, "Yes! I hear you! Everyone, grab the bars!"

The party does so, and it's a good thing too. A massive rolling sandstorm has appeared out of nowhere and absorbs the procession. The cart is thrown into the air and bursts, distributing the characters out around the sand. When the storm passes, the shadow men are in disarray. Two peel off from the main force and charge their rams towards the characters. Luckily, the cart was laden with arms and armor. The characters have just enough time to don their armor before the rams are upon them. A short bloody clash ends with two dead shadow men and no casualties from the party. They flee south, towards the mountain range, their only hope.

Their trek across the desert has left them hungry, but they press on. **Ryuku** takes advantage of his light load and scouts ahead. He finds a triangular marking stone that leads the party to a pass through the mountains. At the start of the trail they find a fallen colum and a statue of a sphinx. **Keppi** recognizes the figure as one that guards entrances, and presses the paws for good luck. Not knowing any better, **Kenji** touches the sphinxes face.

As the sun nears the edge of the sky, the characters venture across a valley between ridges. **Ryuku** spots something moving in the distance. To his horror, he witnesses a gargantuan snake rear it's head out of the bushes. Thinking quick, he ducks to the ground and crawls back to the party. They only have a few moments to deliberate as the snake draws nearer. **Keppi** uses his experience in stealth to hide the party behind a ridgeline, downwind of the snake. The snake passes by their location, but does not find them.

The characters continue on and up a narrow mountain trail. They find a ritual offering - a dead camel with it's throat slit surrounded by offerings. The path splits in two here, one lead up further into the mountain and another leading down. They take the upward path, seeking some kind of shelter.

Fortune favours them as they find a narrow circular tunnel burrowed into the rock. Green grass and moss indicates moisture. The party plan to rest at the entrance, but **Kadian** spots a patch of translucent skin clinging to a jagged rock. It is snake skin, but thicker than normal. Fearing that they have stumbled on the lair of the snake, the party venture further in to confirm their suspicions. The retainers stay behind, fearing the beast.

The tunnel ends in a large bowl shaped cavern - empty. Perched on the lip on the other side of the cave is a skeleton, still wearing it's O-yoroi armor. It wears a katana's sheath but no blade can be seen. **Kadian** retrieves the armor and puts it to better use. Then the party hears a scream from up the tunnel.

## Playtest Notes

- We didn't roll any spellcasters, so I don't know how the magic system goes. The casting system was one of the biggest changes inbetween this and Occluse Again. I should have pre-defined some base types to make sure we had a good spread. The players and I are pretty sure someone is going to die soon though so there will be a chance in the future.

- The combat system diverges from Occluse in that hit points and damage dice are back. In hindsight this creates the exciting dynamism I was looking for. I had neglected that rolling for damage is quite dramatic! Hit points are low and damage dice are quite high, so each roll is a big risk.

- The "circumstances change" on a miss was brilliant. Still taxes my head a little, but I think it's about the right level of dynamism vs effort. A couple times in combat the penalty for missing an attack was simply stepping down the damage die size, which is really easy and applies to all attack situations. I might make it a standard option for when no easy options present themselves for the fight to change dynamically.

- Overall this is a very promising start, we'll see how deadly the game feels as it progresses. There was one fight today and it was over pretty quick, thank god. I feel like these rules are a great step toward delivering on fast and dangerous fights.
