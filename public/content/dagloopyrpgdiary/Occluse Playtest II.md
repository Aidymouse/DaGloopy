# Occluse Playtest II

## Session Report

As **Qann Folecan** receeds into the distance, a burly man approaches the party. His tail is seen first, demarcating him as a tiefling, likely affiliated with House Avia-Damanti of Black Beach. The newcomer is **Zan**, a thug-of-sorts who claims to be employed by some very powerful benefactors. He has been sent to investigate rumors of a fight in Solemtown. Thanks to **Michaels** unmistakable visage, it only took him a day to track them down. **Zan** offers himself as muscle to help them in their task. He seems more brutish tha politically savvy, so the party take him along.

**Rolf** gets cold feet about the killing and abstains to join the party on their quest.

The party approach the farmstead in the afternoon. There they find an old farmer cutting logs. **Michael** goes to investigate while the rest of them hide in tall grass. The old man and his dog, **Young Tayel**, purport to live at the farmstead alone. **Michael** inquires about any else living here but doesn't receive a good answer. The old man is closed up and **Michael** can't get a read on him.

The party stakes out until nightfall with the plan of sneaking in.

After dark, they approach the house. **Ulrius**, **Zeke**, **Zan** and **Phin** peak through the windows, but can't see much other than a general layout of the rooms. **Phin** the farmhand notes that the house is built to a higher standard than most farmer-built shacks. **Michael** approaches **Young Tayel** who is asleep on the front porch. He tries to use the good will she had towards him when they met earlier that day, but something is amiss. The dog is lying down like it's asleep, but its eyes are wide open, watching **Michaels** every move. He reaches out a hand to pet her, but she growls, then stands up and barks.

Then **Michael** feels a sharp pang in his right side, like he's been kicked. An arrow clatters to the ground, fired from some place in the shadows. It's an ambush! He tries to break the farmhouse door down and succeeds, but he falls through with it while **Young Tayel** bites at his arm.

**Zekes** spell *Oozes Incongruous Foot* flattens the tall grass where one of the bow-wielding ambushers is hiding and reveals his position. **Zan** closes the distance with his club but the melee turns south when the bandit lands a lucky hit right in **Zans** neck. Boiling blood coats the ground. The wound is patched by **Zekes** slime while **Phineas** and **Michael** shoot at the hidden brigands.

Indoors, **Ulrius of Death** has smashed through a window, only to find the old man waiting for him inside. The old mans wildly swings his axe, giving **Ulrius** difficulty in his spellwork. Eventually he manages to shatter his *Crystal of the Globular Ghoul* and summon some horrid abomination. After being further empowered with *Leader beware of Flaying*, the ghoul manages to stab its claws right through the old mans ankle. He yields, and the minion is dismissed.

Outside, one bandit is dead and the rest yield in time. One tries to get away but a combo spell from **Zeke** and **Phin** manage to get him and trip him over. The three thieves are gathered in front of the house. **Young Tayel**, the dog, is unconscious but alive.

The old man is not very forthcoming, but the thieves babble that Ruxis Palatine are not the ones in the farmhouse, but rather it is Ruxis Solemn, a different cabal. Palatine sent some people here to trade with Solemn but they haven't been heard from. The old man does confess that Ruxis Solemn are lairing in the farmhouse in the houses expansive wine cellar. 

The party openly debate what must be done with their prisoners. Despite the weakest of the thieves' pleading, the prisoners are all killed.

The house is searched, but it was already been stripped clean long ago. There is a chained door leading to the wine cellar which **Zan** breaks through. **Zeke** communes with the demon inside his book to invoke the power *Farsight of the Underworld*. His deity is greatly displeased at the disturbance, but **Zekes** vision is imbued with the power to see heat signatures. With this power he braves the wine cellar and finds it completely ransacked except for one shelf which is still full. 

The party quickly discern there must be a secret door, and set about investigating the wine bottles. Instead of any known wine vintage, each is marked with a simple sign and name of a saint. **Ulrius of Death** grabs a bottle at random, but it is the wrong one, and he is hit by a poison needle. Drowsiness overtakes him and he falls into a peaceful sleep from which he can not be roused, though he is still alive. **Phin** uses his transmogrified spell *Arrow Nectar* to create an antidote to the poison which revives **Ulrius**. With clever use of a shielding gambeson, **Phin** creates a safe space to try every bottle. He finds the correct one, and a secret door pops open.

The door swings forwards on its own, pushed by a dead body on the other side! The body looks to have died in the last day or two from a sword wound through the back. A trail of blood leads down, deep into a cramped tunnel.

## Playtest Notes

Overall it went very well, no major rules revisions are needed for next game so we're gonna stick with these rules for now.

 - Big fight today! Went on kind of long, but that could be because of some unlucky dice. I think my goal should be fights to take around 3 rounds on average, or at least only 3 rounds until something significant changes. I thought smaller HP values would lead to this but missing attacks really stretches it out. I'm not *entirely* convinced this is a system problem, because it could have been handled by slightly altered scenario design?

 - The combat system started flowing better. I re-framed it as pretty much free-form but you have to spend a resource to do something major. It still coalesces into more-or-less turn by turn, but there were some asynchronous moments. I think I'm getting closer to my ideal combat system - one in which it's freeform but situations can be interpreted into simple mechanics pretty quick, and everyone gets a turn.

 - We got one really good injury hit from an enemy, stabbing **Zan** right through the neck and spouting blood. A bandit got a crossbow bolt through a hand as well.

 - The new spellcasting rules worked great, way easier to adjuticate and led to a couple of unintended benefits:
    - Bartering the mechanics with complexity. Bascially I'd say things like "that'll be complexity 1 if it lasts 1 round, 2 if you want it longer" which I think made it feel more fair.
    - Reasoning about why different aspects of spells cost different amounts revealed some subtle metaphysical attributes of spellcasting in my setting. I like that players can start to get an intuitive sense for why some spells might cost more, but I should probably make sure to try to record that somewhere.

  - It's becoming apparent that casting spells is kind of a lot more fun than attacking with weapons. It's at least a lot more reliable, becuase you can choose to fail casting to hit your attack anyway. This *could* be tackled by me giving fighters more opportunities to hit even on failed rolls (as the action roll rules imply). I might need to create some solid "near miss" bullet points that I can use to adjuticate misses in melee without putting so much cognitive load on me.

 - We had a pretty lengthy post-game discussion about how to make fighting with a crossbow more interesting. In the end, we're not sure it *needs* to be more interesting, it might just be the fate of the crossbow to be simple and undynamic, but it does mean the player who chose the crossbow is just kind of shafted for his choice. I dislike this kind of ivory tower design, but I struggle to figure out a way to make ranged combat consistently interesting.

 - I'm thinking up some more dynamic combat rules. Something along the line of "if you haven't used your action yet, you can declare your action in response to an enemy". Not sure how the mechanics will work out - maybe a contest? The goal is to have a system where you can have a player say something like "I jump out of the way!" in response to an enemys action and it's valid.
